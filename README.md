# WPA Artificial

**Your AI coach for workplace tools**

Learn the tools your team actually uses — with real workplace examples.

## What It Does

WPA Artificial is an AI coach that explains workplace tools like Slack, Excel, Notion, Gmail, Teams, and more in plain English. 

Instead of generic docs, it gives you:
- **Step-by-step instructions** you can follow right away
- **Real-life office scenarios** so it makes sense in context
- **Concise answers** grounded in believable Tuesday-afternoon workplace situations

## How to Use It

1. **Select a tool** from the chip bar at the top. Choose `Any tool` if you're not sure.
   
   Available tools:
   - Slack, Notion, Excel, Google Sheets, Gmail, Outlook
   - Teams, Zoom, Google Docs, Trello, Jira
   - Asana, Figma, Linear, Loom, Calendly

2. **Type your question** in the chat box.

3. **Press "Ask a question"** to get your answer.
